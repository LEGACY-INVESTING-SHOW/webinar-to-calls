# Review: OP.7 Building Your Ops Team: Cleaner, Co-Host, or Property Manager

## First impression (3-5 sentences as the no-context learner)

The first half (jobs, backups, five kinds of access, Airbnb levels, the agreement table) is clear and practical, and I could act on it. Then OP.7.7 drops me into "B1", "B3", "the DSCR quote", "PA.9's diligence", "the base case" and a $29,209 NOI with no idea who Maya and Chris are, which loan they have, or what is already inside that NOI. The math is right, but the one number I'd take away (7.5%) is stated as a general rule on its slide, and the "$81 per hour" figure is overstated, because a messaging-only co-host doesn't take over all 1.5 hours per stay. The ending confused me: the flat messaging service is the only paid option that stays positive, yet Maya and Chris pick "a vendor set with a narrow messaging backup" and nobody says why. The worksheet wants a "weak case" check but has no weak-case line, and I don't have "Sam's PA.4", so I couldn't start Part C.

## Questions I had (numbered; quote the line; say where)

1. Lesson 7.4, last paragraph: "Least-needed access for Cedar Ridge looks like this." What is Cedar Ridge? It isn't introduced until 7.7 ("Maya and Chris live about two hours away"), and never as a house the course follows.
2. Lesson 7.7: "On the DSCR quote, annual debt service (P&I) is $23,637." Then "On the conventional quote ($22,763 debt service)". Do Maya and Chris have two loans, or two offers they haven't chosen between yet? Which one should I treat as real?
3. Lesson 7.7: "NOI: $29,209." What's already subtracted? I only work out that the cleaner and retainer are inside it when "Drop the retainer" adds $1,800 back. What else is in it (insurance, utilities, platform fees)?
4. Lesson 7.7: "These comparisons use the original underwriting (B1). PA.9's diligence added $400 a year on top of B1, the permit renewal. Subtract $400 from every cash-flow figure below." If every figure below is wrong by $400, why aren't the figures below just the corrected ones? Which set do I trust?
5. Lesson 7.7, "18% of gross booking revenue". Does "gross" include cleaning fees? I only learn it does three paragraphs later ("Cleaning fees collected: $74,275 - $63,600").
6. Lesson 7.7, fee-base variant: "212 nights × $300". Where does $300 come from? This is the first time a nightly rate appears.
7. Lesson 7.7, "Pricing owner time": "about 1.5 hours per stay on messages, scheduling, and turnover checks". Option C is "messaging-only". Does it really replace scheduling and turnover checks too? If not, isn't $81 per hour too low?
8. Lesson 7.7: "Use the estimate from 0.3". What is 0.3? And "The other 4 hours a month from 0.3, bookkeeping and pricing": I can't picture where that came from.
9. Lesson 7.7, summary table and "The Cedar Ridge team". The $400/month flat service is the only paid option that stays positive ($772). Why don't Maya and Chris pick it? And what is the "narrow messaging backup" in "a vendor set with a narrow messaging backup"? Is it Chris, or a service?
10. Lesson 7.7, the Cedar Ridge team table, Guest messaging row: "phone on until 10 p.m.; handyman after". How does a guest reach the handyman after 10 p.m. if he has "None" for platform access and "None" for guest data? Does Maya's number forward to him?
11. Same table, Handyman row: "Own lock code, active during job window". The Emergency contact row gives the same handyman "Own lock code" for 2 a.m. calls. Is his code active at 2 a.m. or not?
12. Same table: the cleaner gets "Supplies up to $75 per turnover", and supply replenishment is "Cleaner reports par; Maya orders". Who actually buys supplies? This looks like the "paying twice" case from 7.6.
13. Same table, Bookkeeping row: "Owner views payouts". Maya is "Listing owner" on two rows. Is Chris also an owner on the Airbnb account, or does he log in as Maya?
14. Lesson 7.8, Decision rules: "If the weak case goes negative by more than your reserves can cover for a year". What is the weak case, and what are Cedar Ridge's reserves? And even the vendor set is negative in the weak case ($18,162 - $23,637 = -$5,475). The lesson never says so. Does that mean Cedar Ridge fails rule 2 before any co-host?
15. Lesson 7.8 Part C: "Use your own underwriting from Sam's PA.4." I haven't seen PA.4. What do I put in "NOI with vendor set" if my underwriting doesn't assume a cleaner plus handyman retainer?
16. Lesson 7.8 Part C: "Break-even fee % = cash flow / gross". What if the quote I get is a percentage of nightly revenue, or of payout after platform fees? Is my break-even the same number?
17. Title of the lesson: "Cleaner, Co-Host, or Property Manager". The full manager is described in 7.6 but never priced. How do I judge a manager quote?
18. Lesson 7.4: "When the host's earnings exceed the co-host payouts, co-hosts are paid first." And when they don't? Why does this condition matter to me?
19. Lesson 7.4: "These co-hosts don't appear as co-hosts on the listing". Why should I care whether a cleaner appears on the listing?
20. Lesson 7.1, Owner-only: "the performance review". Review of whom, the team? How often?
