# Review: OP.8 SOPs: Cleaning, Guest Communication & Emergency Response

## First impression (3-5 sentences as the no-context learner)

The promise is clear and useful: write each routine so a backup can run it without calling you. The turnover table and the CO-alarm walk-through are the strongest parts. But I was dropped into a story I don't know. Nobody tells me who Maya and Chris are, what Cedar Ridge is, why there are "6 beds", who is "two hours away", or who the "messaging owner", "after-hours contact", and "handyman" are. The word "owner" means two different things (the person who owns an SOP, and the people who own the house), and that made the missed-clean ladder and the emergency matrix hard to follow. The biggest logic problem is timing of the door code: the message table sends it the day before at 10 a.m., but the ladder and the Sunday example say Maya "releases the code" or "the check-in message" on turnover day after reviewing photos. I can't tell which one to copy. Also, the CO example has the guest call the handyman before 911, which is the opposite of the matrix, and nobody comments on it.
