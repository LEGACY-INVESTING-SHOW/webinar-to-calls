# Review: PA.6 DSCR Loans 101: How They Qualify the Property, Not You

Reviewer stance: a first-time learner. I have never hosted an STR, never bought an investment property, and have not seen any other lesson. I read `parts/12-PA.6.md`, `slides/src/12-PA.6.json` (all notes), and all 20 rendered slides (`tools/shoot.py` reported no overflow).

Math check: I recomputed every Cedar Ridge figure (P&I $1,969.78/mo = $23,637/yr; PITIA $30,684; ratios 2.42 / 1.94 / 1.18 and 1.87 / 1.49 / 0.82; max loans $282,989 at 7.25% and $276,094 at 7.50%; month-24 balance $282,951; penalty $5,659; liquidity $51,970). All of them are correct. The problems are in the explanations, not the arithmetic.

## First impression (3-5 sentences as the no-context learner)

The main idea comes through: the lender's ratio answers "will they lend?" and my own cash flow answers "should I buy?" The slide 7 pairing of "1.87" with "-$456 a month" is the moment it clicks. But I keep running into words that assume I've been here before: "the tracker," "canonical," "Example B7," "Sam's PA.4," "SR.1," "ADR," "pro forma," and a lender called Kiavi that nobody introduces. Two numbers show up from nowhere: NOI of $29,209 and the "canonical refinance" with $15,725 cash out. Then the Lender A / Lender B comparison quietly contradicts the prepayment example I just worked through. By the end I have a good question list, but I'm not sure I could compute the "Monthly P&I" row on my own worksheet.

## Questions I had (numbered; quote the line; say where)

1. 6.1, "The tracker calls this lesson..." What is "the tracker"? It sounds like an internal production document. (Also in slide 2 notes.)
2. 6.1, "Kiavi's own complete guide to DSCR rental loans". Who is Kiavi? Why is this one lender the reference for the whole lesson? The reason ("because its pages are public") only shows up in the slide 20 notes.
3. 6.1, "how much cash you're putting in (the loan-to-value, or LTV)". Isn't LTV the loan as a share of the value, not the cash I put in? If I put 25% down, is my LTV 25% or 75%?
4. 6.0 and slide 1 use "DSCR loan quote" and "A DSCR ratio". What does DSCR stand for? The expansion doesn't come until 6.2 and is never on a slide face (slide 3 notes only).
5. 6.3, "212 booked nights at a $300 ADR, plus cleaning fees". What is ADR? How much are the cleaning fees? 212 x $300 = $63,600, so $10,675 is unexplained. And why is the base case given in nights while the weak case is given as "occupancy to 50%"? I can't compare the two.
6. 6.3, "Base-case NOI is $29,209". Where does this come from? The operating costs ($45,066) only appear a section later, in 6.4. $74,275 - $45,066 = $29,209 should sit right here.
7. 6.3, Ratio 3 "NOI before property tax and insurance / PITIA". Does any lender really use this, or did the lesson build it? It's labeled as one of "three different incomes" a lender might use, but no source says a lender uses NOI-with-tax-added-back.
8. 6.4 heading, "Why a lender ratio above 1.0 doesn't mean you make money". In the table, the NOI-based ratio is above 1.0 exactly when cash flow is positive (1.18 with +$5,572; 0.82 with -$5,475). So is it only the gross ratios that mislead? The lesson never says so, and I came away thinking every ratio lies.
9. 6.4, "and the capital reserve". What is a capital reserve? It's different from the lender "reserves" in 6.7, right?
10. 6.5, "At 7.25% over 30 years, that supports a loan of about $282,989." How do I turn a yearly P&I number into a loan amount? No method, calculator, or spreadsheet function is named. The same gap applies to "Monthly principal and interest is $1,970" in 6.3.
11. 6.5, "supports a loan well above what they asked for. The binding limit there becomes the lender's maximum LTV". How far above? What is a "binding limit"? What is the maximum LTV for Cedar Ridge? (The arithmetic gives about $494,600 of loan capacity from the ratio, far above the 75%-LTV cap of $288,750.)
12. 6.5, "Both loan sizes work out from the canonical inputs." I don't understand what this sentence is telling me.
13. 6.5 uses Maya and Chris's own base-case NOI to size the loan, but 6.6 says the lender picks its own income source and leans low. Would the lender's max loan be even smaller than $282,989?
14. 6.6, "the lower of 110% of appraised market rent or a valid lease rent". Why 110%, and why the lower of the two? (No new fact needed. Just flag it as Kiavi's wording and move on.)
15. 6.6, "The page I could confirm through search doesn't say..." Who is "I"? Why am I reading about the author's search process?
16. 6.7, "The canonical example refinances at the end of year 2 (Example B7)". What refinance? Why would they refinance? What is "cash out"? What is Example B7? The $15,725 has no context in this lesson.
17. 6.7, "Six other terms" beyond the rate, but the second one is "Rate and points". Is the rate one of the six or not?
18. 6.8. Are reserves spent, or do they stay in our bank account? The heading on slide 13 ("keep $51,970 for everything else") makes the $15,342 look spent.
19. 6.9 Q6, "Loan Estimate". Capitalized as if it were a specific form, but it isn't explained until 6.10. Q12, "rate lock". Never explained.
20. 6.10 table, "Lender A (canonical) ... DSCR definition: Gross / PITIA". When was the canonical quote said to use gross / PITIA? 6.3 only says "7.25%, 1 point, 30-year fixed."
21. 6.10 table, Lender A "Max loan on Cedar Ridge: $288,750". Is that a maximum, or just the amount they asked for?
22. 6.10 vs 6.7. The 3-2-1 penalty example in 6.7 was applied to "the DSCR loan" (Lender A's terms). In 6.10, Lender A's prepayment is "ask" and Lender B has the 3-2-1. Which loan carries the 3-2-1?
23. 6.10, "A lower rate with a 5-year prepayment penalty may cost more..." Where did "5-year" come from? Neither lender in the table has a 5-year penalty.
24. 6.11. For rows like "Your base-case NOI (PA.4)", what goes in the "Lender 1" and "Lender 2" columns? Some rows only make sense in one column.
25. 6.11 and slide 19. What if I don't have a property yet? "Fill this in for your own property" assumes I have an address and a PA.4 package.
26. Slide 19 notes, "Each should take under an hour." Can I really get written answers from two lenders in under an hour?

## Confusing or unclear (quote + why)

- 6.1, "how much cash you're putting in (the loan-to-value, or LTV)". This defines LTV as the opposite of what it is. A learner who takes it literally will read "Max LTV can drop" (6.7) backwards.
- 6.2, "Which income: gross booking revenue, a discounted version of it, market rent from the appraiser, or net operating income after expenses." Then 6.3 Ratio 3 uses "NOI before property tax and insurance", which is a different thing from "NOI after expenses." The add-back logic ("so they aren't counted twice") is correct but dense. On slide 5 it sits in 12px grey caption text.
- 6.3, "All figures below are assumptions from the canonical Cedar Ridge example (Example B)." "Canonical" and "Example B" are course-production vocabulary. To me, "canonical" means nothing.
- 6.4 heading, together with the learning outcome's "why a ratio above 1.0 can still leave you losing money". Both are true only of the gross-based ratios. The lesson's own table shows the NOI-based ratio tracks cash flow. That's the most useful insight here, and it is left unsaid.
- 6.4, "NOI already covers tax and insurance". "Covers" (and slide 7's "already inside") are vague. The meaning is "tax and insurance have already been subtracted to get NOI."
- 6.5, "The binding limit there becomes the lender's maximum LTV, not the ratio". "Binding limit" is jargon, and "well above" has no number.
- 6.6, "so it will usually lean lower on income and ignore upside". The slide 10 title hardens this to "it will lean low". That's overconfident for a general claim.
- 6.7, "Search results from Kiavi's site describe its DSCR process as having no cash reserve requirement". The research-process wording ("search results") undermines trust. The slide 11 compression ("Kiavi's site describes none") is ambiguous: none of what?
- 6.7, "Rate and points" listed as one of "six other terms" beyond the rate.
- 6.10, "Read this table in order." The table rows run Rate, Points, Definition..., but the reading order is loan size, upfront cost, exit cost, rate. The table is in the opposite order from the instruction.
- 6.10, the "5-year prepayment penalty" appears from nowhere.
- Sources section: "direct fetch blocked", "see sources-D.md Unresolved", "via WebSearch". Internal research notes in learner-facing text.
