PA.9 and PA.12 call it a "floor" and cite PA.8.6, where it is applied. PA.9 keeps "floor (PA.8.6)" to match PA.12. The PA.8 owner may want one term and one section reference course-wide.

Under PA.8's own rule, both "after seller repairs" ($425) and "final budget" ($7,265) break the buffer at the moment of decision. PA.9 leans on 45 days of saving to get back over it. I softened PA.9's wording ("fits... with only $425 to spare", "still under their floor"), but the two lessons use different pass lines. PA.12 inherits the same pattern.
- **Low-appraisal cash figure.** PA.8.6's prose and decision log say a $375,000 appraisal needs "$7,500 more cash". PA.8.6's own table ($118,112) and PA.9.5 give $7,424, because the point also shrinks. Not changed here. PA.8 could say "about $7,400" or show the point.
- **Where the $4,000 punch list sits in the cash plan.** Lesson 0.3's cash stack and PA.1's cap derivation both reserve a $4,000 punch list. PA.8's Test 3 (seven parts) does not include it. PA.9 therefore pays the $2,955 of repairs out of the $11,020 unallocated cash. If 0.3's $4,000 layer is meant to carry into PA.8, PA.9's cash test would change. Needs a course-level decision; PA.9 figures left as is because PA.12 and OP.11 already use them.
