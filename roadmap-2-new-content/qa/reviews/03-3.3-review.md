# Review: 3.3 Occupancy Taxes & Platform-Verified Registration

Reviewer stance: first-time learner, no STR or investing experience, has not seen any other lesson. Math in every table was re-checked by hand and is arithmetically correct; the issues below are about clarity, labeling, and consistency, not wrong totals.

## First impression (3-5 sentences as the no-context learner)

The cover hook ("A platform that collects some taxes still leaves you owing the rest") is clear, and by slide 13 I understood the main point: in this example I would owe half the tax myself even with Airbnb doing most of the bookings. Getting there was harder than it needed to be. The lesson assumes I know words like "remit", "PMS", "canonical", "A1", "Schedule E", "SR.1", and "Sam", and it switches between annual numbers and a quarter without telling me why. The worked example is the strongest part, but two numbers on the same slide (13% and 13.8%) look contradictory, and the reconciliation slide asks me to trust a $1,133.75 "collected" figure I can't see built. The exercise assumes I already have a property and a quarter of bookings, which I don't.

## Confusing or unclear (quote + why)

- Slide 3 title "One stay can carry four tax layers" over a four-row table of *rules*. A viewer maps the four rows to the four layers in five seconds and gets it wrong.
- Slide 12: "roughly 13.8% on top" (title) vs "$725 x 13%" (box). They are consistent (13% = 6% + 2% + 5% percentage layers; $100.25 / $725 = 13.8%), but the slide never says so, so it reads as an error.
- Slide 10 cell text "Operator (pass-through)" and "Operator (custom tax tool)". It is unclear who collects from the guest vs who files. In those cells the platform collects and the operator files and pays. The column heads ("Airbnb", "Vrbo", "Direct") don't say the cells mean "who remits".
- Slide 8 flow: the red "If it lapses" box follows "Renewed" with an arrow, implying lapse is the next step after renewal. It's a failure branch, not step 4.
- Slide 14 calc: four "owed" lines are added and then "Collected" is subtracted, but no "Owed subtotal = $1,145.75" line appears; the subtotal is buried in the result label. The collected figure has no breakdown on screen.
- Lesson 3.3.8 "Step 1 ... Step 4" collide with the six reconciliation steps in 3.3.7 ("Step 4" vs "step 4"). Also the Step 4 receipts table "Note" column mixes nights ("51 nights", "12 nights") with an event ("3 booked, 1 refunded ($725 removed)"), so Direct's 6 nights never appear.
- Lesson 3.3.6: "Put every expiration date on your calendar with a reminder 60 days out (assumption: my suggested buffer)." The parenthetical reads like an internal tag, not speech.
- Lesson 3.3.8 Step 3: "Half the tax flows through the operator's own accounts in this setup. That's with Airbnb carrying 75% of the stays." The point is good, but "That's with" is a weak connector; the surprise ("even though") is lost.
- Slide 7 bullet 3 "a booking service can't collect a fee until it checks" — "a fee" is ambiguous (see Q13).
- Lesson 3.3.8 line 202 "[Recording note: Preston can add a real example here if he has one.]" is a production note sitting in learner-facing text.
